## 2. Full Store Description (Markdown & Plain Text Format)

```text
FeroCalc is India’s financial calculation utility, fixed deposit (FD) interest rate discovery directory, and personal financial planning companion. Designed specifically for Indian depositors, investors, and salaried professionals, FeroCalc simplifies complex banking mathematics while processing your personal financial figures privately on your device.

★ ON-DEVICE FINANCIAL PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY
• No Consumer Accounts: Zero registrations, passwords, or personal profiles required to use calculators or browse bank rates.
• On-Device Processing: Financial calculations, loan figures, budgets, and saved goals are processed strictly in your device memory and local storage.
• Excluded from Cloud Backups: Personal financial profiles and calculation history are explicitly excluded from Android Google Drive backups (via data_extraction_rules.xml) to prevent off-device leakage.
• Independent Utility: FeroCalc is an independent utility and is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) or any commercial bank. We never ask for bank account numbers, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords.

★ DATA SAFETY & THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURES
• Personal Financial Data: Stays 100% on your device. Never collected, transmitted, or sold.
• Advertisements: Supported by non-intrusive banner ads on the Home screen powered by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects standard device identifiers (Google Advertising ID, App Set ID), IP address (for coarse geographic location estimation), user ad interactions, and diagnostic performance telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
• Rate Alerts (Optional): If you choose to enable verified rate change notifications, an anonymous installation ID and your selected bank preferences are synchronized over HTTPS. You can permanently delete this remote data anytime from the in-app Notification Settings.

★ OFFICIAL VERIFIED FD RATES DIRECTORY
• Compare fixed deposit rates across major public, private, and small finance banks in India (including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Axis, IndusInd, AU Small Finance Bank, Unity SFB, and more).
• Multi-Source Verification: Rates undergo multi-tier automated checks against official bank circulars before being reviewed and published.
• Senior Citizen Premium Support: Easily compare regular vs. senior citizen interest rates (+0.50% p.a. or higher special schemes).
• Special Tenure Detection: Track popular bespoke tenures (400 Days, 444 Days, 501 Days, 701 Days) with exact maturity projections.
• Direct Source Provenance: View official circular publication dates and source references for complete transparency.

★ 18+ POWERFUL FINANCIAL CALCULATORS

1. Fixed Deposits & Banking:
• FD Calculator: Quarterly compounding, simple interest, cumulative and monthly/quarterly payout options.
• RD Calculator: Recurring deposit maturity projections with compounding schedules.
• Compare FDs: Side-by-side comparison of deposit yields, interest payout deltas, and tenure differences between any two banks.

2. Investments & Wealth Planning:
• SIP Calculator: Systematic Investment Plan projections with wealth gain breakdown and visual charts.
• Lumpsum Calculator: Mutual fund one-time investment growth forecasts.
• SWP Calculator: Systematic Withdrawal Plan simulation for post-retirement cash flow.
• CAGR Calculator: Compound Annual Growth Rate computation across multi-year asset performance.
• Compound Interest: Flexible daily, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual compounding.
• Inflation Calculator: Real purchasing power erosion projections using historical CPI benchmarks.
• Dividend Yield Calculator: Stock dividend cash flow estimation.

3. Loans & Borrowing:
• EMI Calculator: Home, car, and personal loan monthly installment projections.
• Amortization Schedule: Complete year-by-year and month-by-month principal vs. interest breakdown with instant PDF export.
• Home Loan Eligibility: Income-based borrowing capacity and FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) estimator.
• Credit Card EMI: True cost of converting credit card debt into installments with processing fees.

4. Government & Retirement Schemes:
• PPF Calculator: Public Provident Fund 15-year maturity with statutory 80C tax deduction tracking.
• SSY Calculator: Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana maturity estimates for the girl child.
• EPF Calculator: Employee Provident Fund accumulation with employer-employee contributions.
• NPS Calculator: National Pension System retirement corpus and monthly annuity estimates.
• Gratuity Calculator: Statutory gratuity estimation under the Payment of Gratuity Act.

5. Taxes & Goals:
• Income Tax Calculator: Comparison of tax liabilities under the Old vs. New Tax Regimes.
• Financial Goal Planner: Set customized targets (Home, Vehicle, Education, Vacation) with monthly savings tracking.
• Financial Health & Insights: Private on-device dashboard assessing emergency fund coverage, debt ratios, and savings health.

★ TRANSPARENT DISCLAIMERS & STATUTORY DISCLOSURES
• Mathematical Estimates: Projections are for educational planning only. Exact bank payouts may vary slightly due to broken-period interest, compounding cycles (quarterly), and leap years.
• DICGC Insurance: Deposits in eligible scheduled commercial banks and small finance banks in India are insured by DICGC (wholly owned RBI subsidiary) up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor per bank in the same right and capacity.
• TDS Provisions: Interest is taxable under Indian income tax law. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is an advance tax withholding mechanism governed by Section 194A (Income-tax Act, 1961) / Section 393(1) (Income-tax Act, 2025). TDS thresholds (₹50,000 / ₹1,00,000 for senior citizens; ₹40,000 / ₹50,000 for regular depositors) do not exempt interest from taxable income.
• Non-Lending Notice: FeroCalc does NOT offer loans, credit lines, deposit acceptance, or money transfer services.
• Google AdMob Disclosure: Supported by clean, non-intrusive banner advertisements clearly demarcated on the home screen.
```
