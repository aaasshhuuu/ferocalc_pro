Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

FinCalc Pro ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (for OTP verification)
  • Profile picture (from Google Sign-In)
  • Device information and unique device identifiers

1.2 Financial Data

We collect financial calculation inputs that you voluntarily enter:

  • Loan amounts, interest rates, and tenures
  • Investment amounts and return expectations
  • Income details for tax calculations
  • Savings and deposit information

Important: All financial calculations are performed locally on your device. We do NOT have access to your actual bank accounts, investments, or financial holdings.

1.3 Usage Data

  • App usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Device type, operating system, and app version

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To sync your calculations across devices (with your consent)
  • To personalize your experience and provide recommendations
  • To send you rate change notifications (with your consent)
  • To improve our app through analytics
  • To display relevant advertisements (free tier)
  • To process subscription payments

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All sensitive data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Authentication tokens are stored in secure device storage (Keychain/Keystore)
  • Cloud-synced data is stored on Firebase/MongoDB with encryption at rest
  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
  • We implement JWT-based authentication with token expiry

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Analytics, Cloud Messaging
  • Google AdMob — Advertising (free tier only)
  • MongoDB Atlas — Cloud database
  • Google Play / App Store — Subscription management

Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share data:

  • With service providers who assist in operating our app
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • With your consent

6. Your Rights

6.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

  • Right to access, rectify, or erase your data
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent

6.2 Indian Data Protection (DPDP Act)

  • Right to access and correct your data
  • Right to data erasure
  • Right to grievance redressal
  • Right to nominate

6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

You can export or delete all your data from within the app (Profile → Privacy Settings → Export/Delete Data), or contact us at privacy@fincalcpro.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

9. Cookies & Tracking

Our mobile app does not use cookies. We use Firebase Analytics and AdMob SDKs which may use device identifiers for analytics and ad personalization.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app or via email.
